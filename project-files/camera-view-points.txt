We will have 4 different view points:

- Orbit camera — rotate around the cable car and zoom in or out- cameraMode = 0 (default)
- Top view — see the scene from above- cameraMode = 1
- Follow camera — follow the cable car along its trajectory- cameraMode = 2
- Cable Car camera — view the scene from inside the cabin- cameraMode = 3
